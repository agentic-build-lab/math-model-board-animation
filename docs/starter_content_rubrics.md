# Starter Content Rubrics

This document preserves the useful starter-rubric ideas from upstream without
copying a separate rubric file for every future content form.

The starting point is intentionally conservative:

```text
composite = average(ER, HP, QL, NA, AB, SR, SAT) * 2.0
```

Each dimension is scored from 0 to 5. The composite range is 0 to 10.

## Shared Dimensions

| Code | Name | General Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ER | emotional_resonance | Does the piece create a specific feeling, identity, or insight? |
| HP | hook_potential | Does the opening stop the target viewer or reader? |
| QL | quotable_lines | Are there lines, commands, code, or claims worth saving or sharing? |
| NA | narrativity | Is there a clear arc that rewards continuing? |
| AB | audience_breadth | How many target viewers can understand why this matters? |
| SR | social_resonance | Does it connect to a current market, technical, cultural, or social signal? |
| SAT | style_depth | Does the voice, humor, teaching personality, or framing add value? |

## Form-Specific Interpretation

### Tutorial Or How-To

- ER means the "aha" moment.
- HP means the problem statement is immediately recognizable.
- QL means reusable code, command, checklist, or configuration density.
- NA means stepwise progression from minimal success to real use.
- SR means the tool or method has current demand.

Tutorial retros should usually wait longer because search and long-tail traffic
matter.

### Long Essay

- ER means a concrete emotional or intellectual recognition.
- HP means title plus opening paragraph strength.
- QL means screenshot-worthy lines.
- NA means readers have a reason to finish.
- SAT means style, irony, or literary device, not forced jokes.

### Short Text Or Thread

- ER, HP, and QL usually dominate.
- NA matters mostly for threads.
- SR captures whether the post names an active public feeling.
- SAT captures tone, irony, and platform-native phrasing.

Short text is useful for fast calibration because the production loop is quick.

### Podcast Or Long Video

- ER means a memorable emotional or intellectual moment.
- HP means the first 30-60 seconds keep the audience.
- QL means clips or quotes that can be repurposed.
- NA means the conversation or episode has an arc.
- SAT means chemistry, humor, or distinctive voice.

Long-form calibration is slower, so every retro should preserve richer notes.

## Cold-Start Discipline

The first 5 samples are mainly data collection:

1. write a blind prediction;
2. publish or produce;
3. collect outcome data;
4. append retro;
5. update observations without rewriting the original prediction.

Do not use a high v0 composite as the only reason to publish. Use it to create
calibrated evidence.
